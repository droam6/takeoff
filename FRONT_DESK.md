# FRONT DESK — the operator's copy-paste scripts

What to send at each step of a job, word for word. Plain language, no jargon, nothing to
compose from scratch at 9pm. Swap the placeholders before sending: `<NAME>` is theirs,
`<EMAIL>` / `<MOBILE>` are ours until the front door exists (`BRAND.md` — the brand name
and tagline live there and only there; sign these however the current brand signs).

The scripts promise only what the product does: same-day numbers, questions instead of
guesses, free re-issue when they answer, a human look when we got it wrong. If a script
ever says more than the documents deliver, the script is wrong — fix it here.

---

## 1. NEW ENQUIRY — first reply to "do you do takeoffs?"

> G'day <NAME>,
>
> Yep — this is exactly what we do. You send us the drawings, we send you back the
> numbers: floor and wall tile room by room, in m², with everything we had to assume
> written down as a question you can answer. You order off it, quote off it, and if
> anything's off you tell us and we re-issue free.
>
> What to send:
> - The plan set as a **PDF straight out of the drawing software** — not a photo or a
>   scan. (Quick check: if you can select a dimension number with your finger or mouse,
>   it's the right kind.)
> - Floor plans for every room you want quoted, and the **internal wall drawings**
>   (elevations) of the bathrooms/ensuite/laundry if you want wall tile too. No wall
>   drawings? No drama — we'll do floors and skirting and add the walls when they turn up.
>
> Turnaround is **same day**. Send it through whenever you're ready.

---

## 2. PLANS RECEIVED — the three job questions

> Got the plans, <NAME> — cheers. Three quick ones so the numbers come out your way:
>
> 1. **What's your trade?** (tiler / painter / something else)
> 2. **Which rooms and surfaces do you want quoted?** (e.g. "both bathrooms and the
>    laundry, floors and walls")
> 3. **How much extra do you allow for cuts?** (most blokes run 10% on a straight lay —
>    say the word if you run different, or if this job's herringbone/diagonal)
>
> Don't stress if you're flat out — **we'll run it either way and flag what we assumed**,
> so you can correct us instead of waiting on us. Numbers back today.

---

## 3. NEW CUSTOMER — add-on line (append to script 2 for a first-timer)

> One more thing since it's your first one with us: the **last page of the PDF you'll get
> is a set-up form** — six quick ticks about how you like to work (lay pattern, rounding,
> box counts and the like). Scribble on it, photo it, send it back once, and every job
> after this comes out your way automatically.

---

## 4. DELIVERY — sending the finished takeoff

> Here's your takeoff, <NAME> — PDF attached.
>
> - **Page 1 is your order box** — quantities with the extra for cuts already in.
> - **Check the tick-box questions on page 1**, and there's an **answer form on page 4**
>   for the smaller things we had to fill in — what we used and what each one's worth,
>   so you can see which are worth your time. Scribble your answers, photo it, send it
>   back — **we re-issue free within 24 hours.**
> - Anything look wrong? Reply and say so — we'd rather fix it today than have you
>   quote off it wrong.
>
> And if this saves you a night at the kitchen table, pass our number to one tradie
> you rate. That's the whole marketing budget.

---

## 5a. PARTIAL — cover text for the "floors first" letter

*Attach the takeoff PDF **and** the `PARTIAL_<job>.md` letter. The letter carries the
detail; this just tops it.*

> <NAME> — good news and a gap, both explained properly in the attached note.
>
> Short version: your floor plans check out, so **your floor and skirting numbers are
> attached and ready to use today**. What the set doesn't have is the internal wall
> drawings of the wet rooms, and wall tile can only be measured off those — we don't
> guess walls. **Send the internal elevations and we'll add every wall to this same
> job**, no extra back-and-forth. Your designer will know the sheets — the note says
> exactly what to ask for.
>
> Reckon we've missed something that's in there? Reply and a human will personally look
> at your file within the day.

---

## 5b. REJECTION — cover text for the "can't measure this yet" letter

*Attach the `REJECTED_<job>.md` letter. Never send this without it — the letter says
exactly what blocked it and what to send instead.*

> <NAME> — straight answer: we can't measure this set accurately yet, and we'd rather
> tell you that than send numbers you can't trust. The attached note lists exactly
> what's blocking it and exactly what to send instead — usually it's getting the PDF
> re-exported straight from the drawing software rather than scanned or printed.
>
> Send that through and your numbers come back **same day**.
>
> And if you reckon we've got this wrong — reply. A human will personally look at your
> file within the day.

---

*Every automated letter already ends with the same appeal line these covers repeat.
That's deliberate: a wrong verdict costs us a look, never costs them the job.*
