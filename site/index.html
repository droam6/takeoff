How it works

  1. 1. Send the plans

    PDF straight from the drawing software, plus three quick questions: your trade, which rooms, how much you allow for cuts. Flat out? We'll run it either way and flag what we assumed.

  2. 2. We measure and double-check everything

    Printed dimensions only — we never scale off a drawing. Every chain of numbers has to add up to the totals on the sheet before we trust it.

  3. 3. Your numbers back same day

    A phone-friendly PDF: what to order on page one, the working underneath. Anything unclear is flagged as a question — never a guess. Answer the questions and we re-issue free.
